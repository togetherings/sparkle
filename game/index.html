    How to play

    Guess the hidden 5-letter word. You have 6 guesses.

    Type a real word and press Enter. Each letter then lights up to give you a clue:

    • S The letter is in the word, in this exact spot.
    • P The letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
    • R The letter is not in the word (or not again).

    Points

    Today's word counts on the scoreboard. You get 1 point for playing, and when you solve it, 3 points plus 1 more for every guess you didn't need. Solving in one guess earns 8 points. Points add up day after day, so playing every day matters most.

    Practice words never count, so try as many as you like.
